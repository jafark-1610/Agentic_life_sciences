# Compatible kinetics and global sharpness

The independent reset replicates share one physical subtype. Their joint law is a rank-three symmetric probability tensor. All three factor matrices have full column rank, giving uniqueness up to subtype permutation and scale; probability normalization fixes scale, and the observable ordering score fixes permutation. The count of physical subtypes is an explicit assumption, rather than a conclusion drawn from tensor rank.

For each subtype, undo the known classifier and reshape the reset distribution into the transition rows starting at phase zero of each module. Summing by endpoint module gives the complete module transition matrix. The bound on total module exchange exit rate places every possible module-generator eigenvalue inside the principal logarithm's imaginary strip. Consequently the module generator is uniquely determined by that module transition matrix.

The two nonconstant Fourier modes of a three-phase module are supported on that module and have eigenvalues `-1.5*(f+b) + R[g,g] +/- i*sqrt(3)/2*(f-b)`. Their exponential gives the observed complex transition eigenvalue. Its magnitude fixes the sum of rates, while its argument fixes the difference only modulo the sampling frequency. All integer choices consistent with positivity and the public rate limits are enumerated. This derives the entire compatible local family; it does not assume that one matrix logarithm supplies all physical generators.
